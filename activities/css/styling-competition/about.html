About Us:

Welcome to Pelagos. We are a pioneering oceanographic research institution dedicated to unlocking the mysteries of the deep sea and advancing global marine conservation. Founded by a team of world-class marine biologists, oceanographers, and robotic engineers, Pelagos bridges the gap between cutting-edge technology and ecological preservation.Our mission is to explore the planet’s least-understood ecosystems, providing critical data to protect marine biodiversity and combat climate change. Through our fleet of advanced autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) and crewed submersibles, we journey into the twilight and midnight zones of the ocean to map the seafloor, discover deep-sea species, and monitor ocean health.At Pelagos, we believe that the survival of our planet depends on the health of our oceans. By collaborating with academic institutions, governments, and conservation organizations worldwide, we turn deep-sea discovery into actionable environmental solutions.
