The Deep

Welcome to Pelagos, the next frontier of Earth exploration. We journey into the deep ocean to protect our planet's future, exploring the twilight and midnight zones where light cannot reach. By deploying next-generation autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) and crewed submersibles, our teams map the uncharted seafloor and gather critical ecological data. This vital research allows us to track deep-sea biodiversity, catalog undiscovered marine species, and provide the global scientific community with the insights needed to combat climate change.

We cannot protect what we do not understand. Because 70% of the Earth is covered by oceans—the vast majority of which remains completely unseen—Pelagos bridges the gap between technology and conservation. Operating at depths of over 10,000 meters, we have already cataloged dozens of new species and established crucial baselines for ocean health. Through global partnerships with governments, academic institutions, and conservation groups, we turn deep-sea discovery into actionable environmental solutions to safeguard our world's most vulnerable ecosystems.
