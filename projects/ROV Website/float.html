What is the FLOAT?
The FLOAT Also Sinks -

Mechanical:

The physical body of the FLOAT is very important. It contains the dry enclosure which is crucial to the machines purpose, and thus the physical aspects are very important. We design all of our mechanical pieces on OnShape, a CAD website which allows us to build our pieces before printing them.

Software:

The software for the FLOAT is arguably the most crucial element to its purpose. The FLOAT needs to sink to the bottom of the pool before rising up, and this process is entirely controlled by the software. We write our software in C++ on Arduino, the same system that is in the FLOAT. Without our software, the float would not be able to fufill its purpose.

Purpose:

Finally, the purpose of the FLOAT is to gather data on the water it sinks and rises in. This technology has real world applications, as gathering data in regards to our oceans becomes ever more aparent with climate change. By sinking down to the bottom of the pool slowly, the FLOAT has time to gather data and send it back to the poolside team. It does the same when rising, and this allows the poolside team to get a complete picture on the data in regards to the water.
