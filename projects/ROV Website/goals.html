Accomplishments and Goals for the Future:
Past Achievements and What We Hope to Do -

This past year, the Francis W. Parker ROV team competed at the highest level possible; Worlds. We had the opportunity to learn and go up against some of the best ROV programs from across the world, including teams from Uganda, Hong Kong, and Greece.

The Worlds tournament is a competition where the best teams compete against one another, and this year, it was hosted in St. Johns, a city in Newfoundland and Labador. We took home 30th place out of 40 teams, and although it isn't the highest ranking, these 40 teams are the best in the world, making us the thirtieth best ROV team in the world.

This year, the Worlds tournament is held in Seattle, and although not as much as a travel destination, our team has aspirations of making it to Worlds back to back. In addition to this, if we are to make it, one of our main goals is to not only do better than last year but also take home some form of award. There are many different awards a team can earn at Worlds, but one of the main ones we want to work towards is the Technical Demonstration award as it would show the progress we've made since last year.
