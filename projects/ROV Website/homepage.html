What is ROV?
ROV stands for Remotely Operated Vehicle -

The main goal of ROV is to inspire and challenge students to learn and creatively apply STEM skills to solving real-world problems in a way that strengthens their critical thinking, collaboration, entrepreneurship, and innovation, and prepares them for the Blue Economy workforce.

The MATE ROV Competition is about student learning. It is designed to be an event that challenges students to apply the physics, math, electronics, and engineering skills they are learning in the classroom to solving problems from the workplace. Mentors (teachers, parents, working professionals) are expected to limit their input to educational and inspiration roles and encouraged to focus on the benefits of the learning process and not simply on "winning" the competition.

H2Operations was founded in 2018 by a small group of students passionate about robotics. We have since grown to be a team with 30 student members and a bigger than ever presence within Francis W Parker school, represnting the community on an international level.
