Banana Bread

Description:

This Banana Banana Bread is a deliciously moist and tender loaf packed with rich, natural banana flavor. Made with overripe mashed bananas, butter, brown sugar, and eggs, it has a soft texture and a warm, comforting sweetness that makes it perfect for breakfast, an afternoon snack, or a simple homemade dessert. The recipe is easy to prepare with everyday ingredients and takes about an hour to bake. For the best results, use very ripe bananas and avoid overmixing the batter so the bread stays light and tender. Enjoy a warm slice on its own, toasted with a little butter, or alongside your favorite cup of coffee or tea.

Banana Bread Ingredients:

  • 2 Cups All Purpose Flour
  • 1 Teaspoon Baking Soda
  • 1/4 Teaspoon Salt
  • 3/4 Brown Sugar
  • 1/2 Cup Butter
  • 2 Large Eggs, Beaten
  • 2 1/3 Cups Mashed Overripe Bananas

Steps:

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Lightly grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl. Beat brown sugar and butter with an electric mixer in a separate large bowl until smooth. Stir in eggs and mashed bananas until well blended. Stir banana mixture into flour mixture until just combined.
  3. Pour batter into the prepared loaf pan.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, about 60 minutes.
  5. Let bread cool in pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
  6. Enjoy!
