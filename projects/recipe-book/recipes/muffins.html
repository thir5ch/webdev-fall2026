Blueberry Muffins

Description:

These Best of the Best Blueberry Muffins are big, soft, and bursting with fresh blueberry flavor, finished with a lightly crisp, sugary topping. The combination of sweet blueberries and tender, buttery muffin batter creates a delicious balance of fruity and comforting flavors in every bite. A portion of the blueberries is crushed into the batter for extra berry flavor, while the remaining whole berries create juicy pockets throughout the muffins. Made with simple ingredients like butter, eggs, flour, buttermilk, and fresh blueberries, this recipe is easy to prepare and makes 12 generous muffins in about 45 minutes. Perfect for breakfast, brunch, or a homemade afternoon snack, these golden muffins are especially delicious when served warm and fresh from the oven.

Blueberry Muffin Ingredients:

  • Nonstick Cooking Spray
  • 1 1/4 Cups White Sugar
  • 1/2 Cup Unsalted Butter
  • 1/2 Teaspoon Salt
  • 2 Large Eggs
  • 2 Cups All-Purpose Flour, Divided
  • 2 Teaspoons Baking Powder
  • 1/2 Cup Buttermilk
  • 1 Pint Fresh Blueberries - Rinsed, Drained, and Patted Dry
  • 2 Tablespoons White Sugar

Steps:

  1. Position rack in the middle of the oven. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). Spray the top of a muffin pan with nonstick spray, and line with paper liners.
  2. Cream together 1 1/4 cups sugar, butter, and salt in a large bowl until light and fluffy. Beat in eggs one at a time.
  3. Mix together 1 3/4 cups flour and baking powder. Beat in the flour mixture alternately with the buttermilk into egg mixture, mixing just until incorporated.
  4. Crush 1/4 of the blueberries, and stir into the batter. Mix the rest of the whole blueberries with the remaining 1/4 cup flour, and fold into the batter. Scoop into muffin cups. Sprinkle tops lightly with sugar.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown and tops spring back when lightly tapped, about 30 minutes.
  6. After taking the muffins out of the oven, allow them to cool before enjoying!
